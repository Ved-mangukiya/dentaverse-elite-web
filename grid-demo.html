📚 How It Works

Understanding the automatic text reduction

Automatic 30% Text on Mobile

Desktop View (100% text):

"Purpose-built VR headsets designed specifically for dental clinics with medical-grade hygiene standards and comfortable ergonomics."

Mobile View (30% text - 1 line only):

"Purpose-built VR headsets designed specifically for dental clinics with medical-grade hygiene standards and comfortable ergonomics."

How to Use:

Option 1: Automatic (Recommended) 
<div class="grid-box">
  <h3>Title</h3>
  <p>Main text - auto-reduces to 1 line on mobile</p>
  <p class="desktop-only">Extra text - hidden on mobile</p>
</div>
Option 2: Manual Control 
<div class="grid-box">
  <h3>Title</h3>
  <p class="hide-mobile">Long desktop text</p>
  <p class="show-mobile">Short mobile text</p>
</div>

Available Classes:

  • .desktop-only or .hide-mobile - Hides on mobile
  • .mobile-only or .show-mobile - Shows only on mobile
  • .mobile-full - Override auto-reduction (show full text)
  • .text-adaptive - Smart text reduction
  • .mobile-truncate - Cut with ellipsis (...)

✨ Result:

  • ✅ Mobile boxes 70% smaller
  • ✅ Faster loading and scrolling
  • ✅ Better mobile UX
  • ✅ Professional appearance
  • ✅ No horizontal scroll
