How We Started

A Problem We Lived Ourselves

Our founder, Dr. Jayshri Harini Dharanikumar, spent years watching patients grip the armrests, cancel appointments, and avoid necessary treatment — not because of the procedure, but because of fear.

She assembled a team of clinicians, technologists, and patient advocates with one shared belief: dental anxiety is a solvable problem. The answer wasn't more sedation or longer appointments. It was immersion.

Today, Dentaverse provides VR headsets and curated therapeutic content to dental clinics across the UK — turning the most dreaded appointment into a moment of genuine calm.

"Relax Your Mind, Fear Left Behind."
— Dentaverse Founding Principle

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To design and deliver advanced VR headsets specifically for dental clinics, featuring customised therapeutic content — educational videos, cartoons, and immersive environments — to eliminate patient fear and improve clinical satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become a global leader in immersive healthcare technology — transforming the future of dental care through innovative VR solutions that improve patient comfort and emotional well-being across the globe.
