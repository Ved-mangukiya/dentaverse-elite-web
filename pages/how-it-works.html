The Dentaverse Method

A Journey of Six Phases

From first contact to post-treatment calm — every step is designed with the patient's mind in focus.

Phase 01

Pre-treatment Education

We familiarize the patient with the VR equipment and virtual concept, setting the stage for total relaxation before they even enter the clinic chair.

Clinical Validation

The Science of Neural Distraction

Dentaverse utilizes "Cognitive Gate Control Theory." By saturating the visual and auditory pathways with peaceful stimuli, we effectively block anxiety-related signals from reaching the primary consciousness.

This results in higher patient cooperation, fewer procedural interruptions, and a significantly elevated clinic reputation.

Validated through clinical tests measuring cortisol levels and heart rate variability.

Immersive environments create a psychological effect where procedures feel 40% faster.
