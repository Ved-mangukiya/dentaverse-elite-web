How We Started

A Problem We Lived Ourselves

Our founder, Dr. Jayshri Harini Dharanikumar, spent years watching patients grip the armrests, cancel appointments, and avoid necessary treatment — not because of the procedure, but because of fear.

She assembled a team of clinicians, technologists, and patient advocates with one shared belief: dental anxiety is a solvable problem. The answer wasn't more sedation. It was immersion.

Today, Dentaverse provides VR headsets and curated therapeutic content to dental clinics across the UK — turning the most dreaded appointment into a moment of genuine calm.
